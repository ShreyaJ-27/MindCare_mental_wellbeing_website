🧠 Understanding Anxiety

What it is and how to manage it

Anxiety is your body's natural response to stress. It's that feeling of fear or worry about what's coming next.

Common Signs:

  • • Racing thoughts or constant worry
  • • Difficulty concentrating
  • • Restlessness or feeling on edge
  • • Physical symptoms (rapid heartbeat, sweating)
  • • Trouble sleeping

Coping Strategies:

Deep breathing: Try the 4-7-8 technique—breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 7, exhale for 8.

Grounding: Use the 5-4-3-2-1 method—name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

Movement: Physical activity helps release anxiety. Even a short walk can help.

Talk it out: Share your feelings with someone you trust.

💙 Managing Depression

Understanding and coping

Depression is more than just feeling sad. It's a persistent feeling that affects how you think, feel, and handle daily activities.

What Depression Might Feel Like:

  • • Persistent sadness or emptiness
  • • Loss of interest in activities you used to enjoy
  • • Fatigue or lack of energy
  • • Changes in sleep or appetite
  • • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
  • • Feelings of worthlessness or guilt

Small Steps That Help:

Start tiny: Set achievable goals like getting out of bed, taking a shower, or opening your curtains.

Stay connected: Reach out to one person, even if it's just a text.

Be kind to yourself: Depression is not your fault. Healing takes time.

Seek support: Talk to a counselor, therapist, or trusted adult. Professional help makes a real difference.

😴 Sleep & Mental Health

The connection you need to know

Sleep and mental health are deeply connected. Poor sleep can worsen anxiety and depression, while good sleep supports emotional resilience.

Why Sleep Matters:

During sleep, your brain processes emotions, consolidates memories, and recharges. Lack of sleep affects mood, focus, and stress levels.

Sleep Hygiene Tips:

  • • Keep a consistent sleep schedule
  • • Create a calming bedtime routine
  • • Limit screen time 1 hour before bed
  • • Keep your room cool, dark, and quiet
  • • Avoid caffeine in the evening
  • • Try relaxation exercises before sleep

📱 Social Media & Wellbeing

Finding healthy balance

Social media can be both connecting and overwhelming. Learning to use it mindfully protects your mental health.

Signs It's Affecting You:

  • • Feeling worse about yourself after scrolling
  • • Comparing yourself to others constantly
  • • FOMO (fear of missing out)
  • • Difficulty focusing on real-life activities
  • • Sleep disruption from late-night use

Healthier Habits:

Set boundaries: Use app timers to limit daily use.

Curate your feed: Unfollow accounts that make you feel bad. Follow uplifting content.

Take breaks: Regular digital detoxes help reset your relationship with social media.

Real connections: Prioritize face-to-face time with friends.
